# Elsa 4.1

*[English version below](#english)*

Analisi elastica di strutture piane di travi con il metodo degli spostamenti
e il metodo delle forze: vincoli, sconnessioni, carichi distribuiti,
cedimenti e deformazioni impresse; deformata e diagrammi di N, T e M;
schema 0, schemi Xi = 1 e incognite iperstatiche. Fra gli esempi: strutture
chiuse, carichi autoequilibrati e stati di coazione.

Funziona nel browser su PC, tablet e telefono, si installa come app e
funziona anche senza connessione.

**Apri l'app: <https://leonardoleonetti.github.io/elsa/>**

- Android e PC (Chrome, Edge): menu del browser › *Installa app*
- iPhone e iPad (Safari): Condividi › *Aggiungi alla schermata Home*

# Elsa 4.1 (English)

Elastic analysis of plane beam structures with the displacement method and
the force method: supports, internal releases, distributed loads, support
settlements and imposed strains; deformed shape and N, V, M diagrams;
system 0, Xi = 1 systems and redundants. The examples include closed
structures, self-equilibrated loads and states of self-stress.

It runs in the browser on computers, tablets and phones, can be installed
as an app and also works offline.

**Open the app: <https://leonardoleonetti.github.io/elsa/Elsa_EN.html>**

- Android and computers (Chrome, Edge): browser menu › *Install app*
- iPhone and iPad (Safari): Share › *Add to Home Screen*

## PC version

**[Download Elsa_EN.html](https://github.com/leonardoleonetti/elsa/releases/latest/download/Elsa_EN.html)**:
